Local technical coordination for vessels

When a technical issue needs attention during a Thessaloniki call, the first practical step is to describe the equipment, symptoms and access window clearly. SKG Port & Ship Agency coordinates requested technical attendance with local marine engineers and relevant trades. Attendance depends on the issue, port conditions and suitable personnel availability; the agency does not operate its own workshop or employ the technicians directly.

Support can be requested for auxiliary engines, generators, pumps, boilers and hydraulic or pneumatic systems. Depending on the fault and agreed scope, local marine engineers can be asked to assess, troubleshoot, maintain or undertake repair work. We coordinate the request and communication; the attending specialist is responsible for its technical assessment and the work it performs.

Mechanical, electrical and network services

Related attendance may include pipefitter or network-fitting services, electrical troubleshooting, and support for fiber-optic, network or computer issues. Equipment details, alarms, photographs and access requirements help identify the specialist needed. For equipment requiring parts, testing or further work, the scope and practical constraints need to be established with the technical provider before any work is agreed.

  • Auxiliary engines, generators, pumps and boilers.
  • Hydraulic and pneumatic systems.
  • Pipefitter and network-fitting attendance.
  • Electrical troubleshooting, fiber optics, networks and computers.

Confirming the right attendance

For an enquiry, include the vessel name, port-call timings and location, a contact on board, a description of the affected system and the issue observed, and whether the equipment is operational or isolated. Share any relevant drawings, model information, alarms or prior troubleshooting notes if available. Also identify the desired outcome and the time window in which a visit may be possible. We will discuss what can be coordinated and confirm scope and availability before attendance.

Repairs are not guaranteed in advance: the cause, parts, access and work duration can only be assessed in context. No work should begin without the vessel’s authorization and applicable safety arrangements. For wider call coordination, see ship agency in Thessaloniki; for crew matters, see crew change support.

Submit the requested equipment and timing details through our technical support quote form. We will review the request and confirm whether local coordination is available for the reported scope.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have your own workshop or direct-employed repair team?

Our role is to coordinate requested attendance with local marine engineers and trades. We do not claim to operate a workshop or employ the specialist personnel directly.

Can you guarantee a repair during the port stay?

No. A repair depends on diagnosis, parts, access, timing and the technical provider’s assessment. We can discuss the reported issue and confirm the scope and availability of attendance before it is arranged.

Describe the issue

Include the equipment, symptoms and available attendance window. Scope and availability are checked before a visit.
