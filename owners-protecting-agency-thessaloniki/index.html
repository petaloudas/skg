An independent view of the port call

When a charterers’ agent is nominated, an owner or manager may want an independent local presence focused on its own interests. SKG Port & Ship Agency provides Owners’ Protecting Agency (OPA) support at the Port of Thessaloniki. The role is to monitor the requested aspects of cargo operations, keep the principal informed and help maintain a clear contemporaneous understanding of events.

The scope is agreed with the owner or manager before the call. It may include attendance around cargo operations, independent monitoring of the sequence and conditions observed, and oversight of the Statement of Facts (SOF). It does not replace the vessel’s records, contractual interpretation, or the decisions of the owner and its advisers.

Statement of Facts oversight and dispute support

The SOF records events and timings connected with a port call and can be important when operations or time are later reviewed. OPA support can include checking the document against the information available to the attending representative, drawing attention to entries that appear incomplete or inconsistent, and communicating observations to the principal. If a disagreement arises, the owner or manager receives the relevant factual observations so it can decide how to proceed.

Any formal reservation, protest or contractual position remains for the owner, master or their appointed advisers to determine. The value is independent monitoring, careful documentation and timely communication within the agreed instructions.

Agreeing practical attendance

To assess a request, share the vessel and cargo details, expected arrival and operation schedule, the nominated agent’s role, relevant instructions, and the contact person ashore or on board. If available, provide the documents or reporting format the principal wants followed. We can then discuss the proposed coverage, likely attendance needs and any information to be clarified before confirming scope and availability.

  • Independent cargo-operation monitoring at Thessaloniki.
  • SOF oversight and communication of observed discrepancies.
  • Protection of owners’ interests when charterers’ agents are nominated.
  • Updates to the principal within the agreed reporting scope.

Frequently asked questions

Does OPA replace the appointed ship agent?

No. OPA is an independent service for the owner or manager. The appointed agent continues its own role; the precise scope of both appointments should be clear to the parties involved.

Can you guarantee that a cargo or SOF dispute will be resolved?

No. We can monitor the agreed activity and report factual observations. The owner or manager decides how to address a disagreement, including whether to seek legal or contractual advice.
