Who receives your enquiry

SKG Port & Ship Agency is the trading identity of FOC ΜΟΝΟΠΡΟΣΩΠΗ Ι.Κ.Ε. Contact us at office@skgportagency.gr about your enquiry or the use of information you provide.

Information submitted

The contact form sends your name, email address, subject and message. The quotation form also sends the company, vessel name and requested service, plus any phone number, IMO number and estimated arrival time you choose to provide. The form page and any campaign identifiers present in the link (source, medium and campaign) are included with the enquiry so we can understand where it came from.

Do not send passport copies, crew medical records, passwords, payment-card data or other sensitive personal or security information through these forms. If documents are needed later, contact us to agree an appropriate method.

Purpose

We use the submitted information to respond to your request, clarify scope, prepare a quotation and coordinate the services you ask about. Sending a form does not make a booking, authorize a payment or subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Delivery through FormSubmit

The forms send the entered information over HTTPS to FormSubmit, an external form-to-email service, for delivery to our company mailbox. FormSubmit also receives the technical information involved in handling the request. Review the provider’s privacy policy for its processing practices.

A successful submission notice means the delivery service accepted the enquiry; it does not confirm that a member of our team has read it. For urgent operational matters, please telephone us using the contact details on the contact section.

Cookies and measurement

The website’s enquiry code does not set analytics or advertising cookies or load Google Analytics or Google Ads tags. Campaign labels may be carried between links during your visit without cookies or browser storage and are only sent to the delivery service when you submit an enquiry. The hosting and form-delivery providers may process technical request information as part of providing their services.

Your requests

Contact office@skgportagency.gr to ask about the information you have submitted, request correction or deletion, or exercise other applicable data-protection rights. Some business correspondence may need to be retained where required for an ongoing service, legal obligations or the establishment or defence of legal claims.
