Keep crew movements coordinated

A crew change involves more than a joining or leaving date. Travel information, vessel movements, shore access and individual arrangements need to be considered together. SKG Port & Ship Agency coordinates crew changes at the Port of Thessaloniki and can assist with the associated arrangements requested by the vessel, owner or manager. Support is available for day and night operations; each attendance and service is subject to confirmation for the specific call.

For an initial request, provide the vessel name, expected arrival and berth information when known, crew list and movement details, nationality and travel itinerary, plus the principal’s preferred point of contact. Early and accurate details help identify what arrangements need to be checked. If timings or travel plans change, updated information is important so that the parties involved can review the plan.

Travel, shore and welfare arrangements

Depending on the request, we can coordinate visa and OKTB arrangements, shore passes, airport transfers and hotel accommodation. We can also coordinate medical attendance and help arrange personal items, medicines or technical supplies for crew. Cash to Master can be requested as part of the service scope. These are coordination services: entry permissions and visa decisions belong to the competent authorities, medical advice is provided by medical professionals, and supplier or transport arrangements depend on confirmed availability.

  • 24/7 crew-change coordination, subject to confirmation of the operation.
  • Visa and OKTB arrangements and shore-pass coordination.
  • Medical attendance, hotel accommodation and airport transfers.
  • Personal items, medicines, technical supplies and Cash to Master requests.

Practical information before the movement

For each crew member, share the relevant travel itinerary and documents through the appropriate secure channel agreed with your team. Let us know whether the person is joining or signing off, and provide the expected vessel and transfer schedule. Please identify any medical or mobility needs and any items requiring special handling. We can discuss the steps to coordinate locally and confirm the available scope; we do not promise that a visa, OKTB, shore pass or other approval will be granted.

Thessaloniki is our service location. If your enquiry also includes a wider vessel call, see ship agency support; technical attendance can be requested through marine technical support. To outline a crew movement, use the crew support quote form.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee a crew member’s visa or entry approval?

No. We can coordinate the requested visa-related arrangements and communicate requirements, but only the competent authorities decide on visas and entry permission.

What details help you coordinate a crew change?

Send vessel and timing details, joining or sign-off status, crew and itinerary information, and any requested hotel, transfer, medical or supply arrangements. Please flag changes promptly.
