One coordinated port call

At Thessaloniki, a vessel call brings together schedules, documentation, cargo activity and practical requirements on board. SKG Port & Ship Agency coordinates the work around the vessel’s arrival, port stay and departure, helping owners and managers keep the local arrangements connected. The details are shaped around the vessel, its operation and the instructions received from the principal.

Before arrival, we can coordinate the required port formalities and documentation with the relevant parties, and keep the operational picture aligned as estimated timings change. During the stay, agency support can extend to cargo supervision, provisions and bunkering coordination. As departure approaches, we coordinate the remaining formalities and communicate the status of arrangements to the vessel’s representatives.

Support through arrival, stay and departure

Clear information at the outset makes the call easier to coordinate. Owners, managers or operators can share the vessel particulars, expected arrival, berth or cargo details where known, and the services being considered. We can then confirm the practical scope, information required and availability for the specific call. Where an item depends on port, supplier or authority processes, that dependency is discussed rather than assumed.

  • Vessel arrival, stay and departure coordination at the Port of Thessaloniki.
  • Coordination of customs and other port or legal documentation formalities.
  • Cargo-operation supervision and communication around the port call.
  • Provisions and bunkering arrangements coordinated to the vessel’s request.

Local coordination, clear communication

A ship agent is a practical link between the vessel, its owner or manager and the local port-call participants. We focus on the requested scope, share relevant updates and help clarify what is needed next. Agency work is not a substitute for decisions reserved to the vessel, owner, authorities or service providers; the responsible party remains involved where its approval or action is required.

Our service is for the Port of Thessaloniki only. If you need a conventional ship agency appointment, or want to add a specific service to an existing call, send the vessel details for a quote. We will confirm scope and availability before attendance.

Frequently asked questions

What should I provide for an initial agency enquiry?

Share the vessel name and particulars, your role and contact details, estimated arrival, known cargo or port-call requirements, and any services requested. Information can be updated as the schedule develops.

Can you coordinate supplies or bunkering as part of the call?

Provisions and bunkering coordination can be requested. Product, quantities, timing, supplier arrangements and port requirements need to be reviewed for the particular vessel and call.

Planning a port call?

Tell us what the vessel needs at Thessaloniki. Scope and availability are confirmed before attendance.
